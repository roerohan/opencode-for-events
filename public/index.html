Welcome to OpenCode for Events

Get started with OpenCode for your event. Each team has a credit allocation to use during the event.

Event Access: Your team has been allocated a specific credit limit. Once exhausted, access will be automatically restricted.
  • Install Opencode
    Follow our installation guide here.
    If you've installed OpenCode previously, make sure you are on a recent version via opencode upgrade or via your package manager.
  • Authenticate via Terminal
    Run this command in your terminal:
    opencode auth login https://opencode-for-events.roerohan.com
  • Start Using Opencode
    Once authenticated, you're ready to use all Opencode features!
  • Check Your Team's Credit Status
    Your event organizers can check your team's remaining credits. Use OpenCode responsibly to make the most of your allocation throughout the event.

FAQ

How do credit limits work?

Each team is allocated a fixed credit limit (e.g., $20) for the duration of the event. All team members share this pool. Once your team exhausts the credits, access will be automatically restricted.

How do I check my authentication status?

Run opencode auth status in your terminal. If expired, re-run opencode auth login https://opencode-for-events.roerohan.com.

What if my team runs out of credits?

Contact your event organizers if you need additional credits. Access will be blocked when the team limit is reached to ensure fair distribution across all participants.

Where can I learn more about OpenCode?

Check out the official OpenCode documentation for guides, configuration options, and advanced features.
